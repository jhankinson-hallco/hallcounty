Steps to use SAML

Download new barracuda client version because old version does not have SAML as an option.
Open up the client and on the top left corner, click the sandwich (three bars; see image below)
Click on "Manage Profiles"
Select the profile user created to use the vpn.
Click on "Edit Profile"
Under General, Authentication Method > click "Username and Password." Options will appear.
Select SAML
Then click "Save" at the bottom.
Go back to the client (where you put in your username and password)
You should only see the option to connect and this option will take you to through the process to use SAML and MFA
